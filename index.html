01 · Background

About me

I'm a Mechanical Engineering student at Iowa State University, with professional experience spanning the robotics, automation, and construction industries — working in robotic design, automation equipment integration, and project management.

This past summer, I worked across both the robotics and manufacturing industries with a Stealth Startup and Premier Tech. At the Stealth Startup, I worked on UMI hardware projects, including a 1-DOF claw, a 21-DOF hand, and the mass manufacturing of company-internal products. As a Project Engineer at Premier Tech, I served as an automation equipment integrator, using Autodesk Revit to plan integrations across industrial facilities nationwide.

On campus, I've held roles across Triangle Fraternity and Cyber Robotics at Iowa State. Within Cyber Robotics, I serve as President for the 2026–2027 season while also serving as Mechanical Team Lead, and served as Executive Competition Director this past semester. Within Triangle, I was elected twice as New Member Educator, welcoming 40+ members into the fraternity.
